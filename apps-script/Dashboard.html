What the statuses mean
Needs ReviewThe AI wasn't sure which project it belongs to. Open it and correct it.

In ProcoreDownloaded and put through SmartBuild / Procore for approval.
PaidConfirmed paid in Nexus. One of the two ways an invoice ends.
CanceledRejected outright and will never be paid. The other way it ends.
DuplicateThe same invoice as another row. It shares that row's PDF, so never move its file.
Not an InvoiceA statement, quote or other document that arrived at the billing inbox.
